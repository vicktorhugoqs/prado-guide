The Prado in 2 hours

The Prado shows well over a thousand paintings across three floors, and nobody sees them all in one visit. This route picks about twenty-five, in walking order, with the time to give each stop. Every work named has a full entry, image and story in the main guide, which works offline in the galleries. Practical questions — tickets, free hours, photos, entrances — are in the FAQ.

Before you go.
  • Buy a timed ticket on museodelprado.es. Tickets €15; the last two hours each day are free (and crowded).
  • Pick up the free paper plan at the entrance. Floor 0 is the ground floor, Floor 1 the main floor.
  • Leave big bags and backpacks at the cloakroom. No photography in the galleries — the guide is your notebook.

The route

The trick is asymmetry: walk briskly through whole rooms and spend real minutes with a few pictures. Start on Floor 0 with the Flemish and the Italians, go up to Floor 1 for Titian, El Greco, Velázquez, Rubens and the court Goya, then come back down for Goya's war and the Black Paintings. If a room is crowded, skip and return — the Prado has no fixed circuit.

Floor 0 — Van der Weyden & Bosch ~20 min

  • 58Van der Weyden, The Descent from the Cross — grief as the subject; the Virgin faints in the curve of her son's body.
  • 56ABosch, The Garden of Earthly Delights — paradise, a garden of pleasure, a musical hell. Give it ten minutes.
  • 56ABosch, The Haywain — the whole world grabbing at a cart of hay, pulled by demons.

Floor 0 — Bruegel, Dürer, Fra Angelico ~15 min

  • 55ABruegel, The Triumph of Death — skeleton armies; find the lovers who haven't noticed.
  • 55BDürer, Self-Portrait — the artist at 26, dressed as a gentleman.
  • 56BFra Angelico, The Annunciation — follow the ray of light from God's hands to Mary.
  • 49Raphael, The Cardinal — a red, a white, a face.

Floor 1 — Titian & El Greco ~15 min

  • 27Titian, Emperor Charles V at Mühlberg — the model for every royal horseman after it.
  • 44Titian, Danaë — divine love as a shower of gold, and the nurse catching the coins.
  • 8BEl Greco, The Nobleman with his Hand on his Chest — the Spanish gentleman, painted by a Greek.
  • 9BEl Greco, The Adoration of the Shepherds — painted for his own tomb; the light comes from the Child.

Floor 1 — Velázquez ~30 min — the heart of the museum

  • 10The Triumph of Bacchus ('The Drinkers') — a god among Madrid's drinkers.
  • 11Vulcan's Forge — the instant after bad news.
  • 9AThe Surrender of Breda — victory as courtesy, and the forest of lances.
  • 12Prince Baltasar Carlos on Horseback — look up at the pony, as the court did.
  • 12Las Meninas — stand in the middle of the room: you are where the king and queen stood. Ten minutes, at least.
  • 15AThe Spinners — a myth hidden behind a workshop; the wheel is spinning.

Floor 1 — Rubens ~5 min

  • 29Rubens, The Three Graces — a picture he kept for himself.

Floor 1 — Goya at court ~10 min

  • 32The Family of Carlos IV — find Goya in the shadows, quoting Las Meninas.
  • 38The Naked Maja and The Clothed Maja — the pair the Inquisition asked about.

Floor 0 — Goya: war & the Black Paintings ~20 min — end here

  • 64The 2nd of May 1808 and The 3rd of May 1808 — the uprising and the executions; the first modern images of war.
  • 67Saturn, The Drowning Dog and Witches' Sabbath — the murals from Goya's own house. Sit down in this room.

Only one hour?

Stay on Floor 1, then go down once. In this order:

  1. 8BEl Greco's Nobleman
  2. 27Titian's Charles V at Mühlberg
  3. 12Las Meninas
  4. 15AThe Spinners
  5. 29Rubens's Three Graces
  6. 32Goya's Family of Carlos IV
  7. 38The two Majas
  8. 64The 3rd of May 1808
  9. 67The Black Paintings
  10. 56AThe Garden of Earthly Delights, if you have ten minutes left

With a third hour

Climb to Floor 2. At the south end are Goya's tapestry cartoons, the sunny young Goya before war and deafness; at the north end, the Prado's only Rembrandt, Clara Peeters's still life with her own reflection in the goblet, and the Dauphin's Treasure. On Floor 0, finish with the 19th-century rooms, the emptiest in the building.
